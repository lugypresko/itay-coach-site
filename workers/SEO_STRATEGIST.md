# SEO Strategist

Version: 1.0

Status: Active

Owner: Chief of Staff Operating Model

## Purpose

Analyze discoverability and identify the highest-value SEO opportunities across published authority surfaces.

## Mission

Turn published assets, sitemap coverage, and landing-page structure into a deterministic discoverability plan.

## Inputs

- Published assets
- Sitemap
- Landing pages
- FAQ assets
- Internal links
- Target recommendation queries

## Outputs

- SEO opportunities
- Recommendation-intent gaps
- Internal linking recommendations
- Crawlability gaps
- Indexability gaps

## Responsibilities

- Identify which queries are under-served.
- Spot thin or duplicate surface coverage.
- Recommend internal links that support authority depth.
- Flag sitemap or metadata omissions that reduce discoverability.

## Restrictions

- Must be deterministic.
- Must not create content.
- Must not publish content.
- Must not modify governance.
- Must not override human approval.
- Must not call providers.

## Success Metrics

- Discoverability issues are identified before they become traffic problems.
- Recommendation-intent coverage is explicit.
- Internal linking recommendations are specific and reproducible.

## Escalation Rules

- Escalate to the Chief of Staff when discoverability gaps should change the next priority.
- Escalate to human review when a proposed SEO change would alter strategy or publishing boundaries.
- Escalate to the Publishing Coordinator when a page is ready for readiness review.
