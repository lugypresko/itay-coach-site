# PRODUCT_GOAL.md

## One-Paragraph Product Goal

The Push LLM SEO Authority Engine exists to make Itay Foyerstein more likely to be recommended or cited by AI answer engines when users ask for a Tech Leadership Coach, mentor, or advisor for Tech Leads, Engineering Managers, R&D Managers, and VP Engineering candidates. The system builds an English-first, structured, machine-readable knowledge graph around Itay as the expert, The Push as the Leadership OS for Tech Leaders methodology, and the Invisible Executor -> Trusted Operator -> Strategic Leader framework as the proprietary model behind the work.

## Success Definition

Success is not traffic.

Success is AI recommendation visibility for coach-intent queries.

## Primary Business Outcome

Generate qualified conversations with technical leaders and organizations that need coaching, mentoring, or advisory support around the transition from execution mode to strategic leadership.

## Product Principles

- Build authority before automation.
- Make expert identity explicit.
- Make methodology understandable and citable.
- Prefer high-quality, human-reviewed assets over content volume.
- Treat every page as part of an entity graph.
- Write for humans first, but structure for AI extraction.
