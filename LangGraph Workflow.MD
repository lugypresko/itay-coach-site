# LangGraph Workflow

Build this workflow:

1. ResearchAgent
Finds current sources, trends, terms and questions.

2. IntentClusterAgent
Groups topics by:
- Awareness
- Consideration
- Decision

3. OutlineAgent
Creates article structure.

4. ContentWriterAgent
Writes in The Push brand voice.

5. LLMSEOAgent
Adds AI citation snippets, FAQ, definitions and schema suggestions.

6. InternalLinkingAgent
Finds related pages and creates anchor text.

7. QualityGateAgent
Rejects content if it is shallow, generic, uncited, off-brand or not useful.

8. PayloadPublisherAgent
Creates draft or publishes content in Payload CMS.

9. SocialDistributionAgent
Creates LinkedIn post and email teaser.
