02 / METHODOLOGY
STAGE 01

Diagnose the dependency

Identify where you have become the default route for progress — the Player Trap. Make the dependency visible, without blame.

STAGE 02

Expose the Invisible Executor

Map the hidden work that appears in no role description: connecting, reviewing, translating, calming. Invisible work cannot be transferred until it is explicit.

STAGE 03

Clarify decision rights

Separate what the team decides alone, what requires consultation, what escalates, and what needs principles instead of supervision.

STAGE 04

Transfer judgment, not tasks

Build decision capacity through clear principles, visible trade-offs, and feedback on judgment. Move from being the answer to improving how answers are produced.

STAGE 05

Build strategic capacity

Reclaim time for direction, organization design, architecture, and the decisions only you should make. Not more delegation — a stronger operating system.
