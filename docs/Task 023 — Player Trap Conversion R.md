Task 023 — Player Trap Conversion Redesign
Objective

Transform the current site from an authority/coaching website into a diagnostic-first conversion funnel.

Current problem

The site sells coaching.

The market is not looking for coaching.

The market is looking for an explanation:

"Why am I working harder than ever but still not perceived as the next-level leader?"

The site currently focuses on:

- AI Leadership
- AI-first management
- Program description
- Benefits

The site should focus on:

- The Player Trap
- Invisible Executor
- Leadership evolution
- Self-diagnosis
- Identity tension

Primary Goal

Increase Player Trap Test starts.

Secondary Goal

Increase completed tests.

Tertiary Goal

Increase Diagnosis Call bookings.

------------------------------------------------

PHASE 1 — HERO REDESIGN

Replace current hero completely.

Current message:

Stop Drowning in AI. Start Leading It.

New message:

Headline:

You've already proven you're the strongest player.

That might be exactly what's holding you back now.

Subheadline:

The Player Trap Test reveals whether your strongest leadership habits are quietly turning you into the bottleneck.

CTA:

Take the Test

Microcopy:

3 minutes.
Built for tech managers.
No fluff.

------------------------------------------------

PHASE 2 — DIAGNOSTIC SECTION

Replace "This Is What's Happening Right Now"

Create section:

4 Signs You're Stuck in the Player Trap

Card 1

The team waits for you too often.

Card 2

You solve problems faster than the system can.

Card 3

Your workload grows with every promotion.

Card 4

Everyone values you.
Not everyone sees you as the next-level leader.

------------------------------------------------

Create visual framework section.

Title:

The Evolution of a Tech Leader

Visual progression:

Star Player
↓
Captain
↓
System Builder
↓
Pre-Promoted Leader

Each stage should have:

- short description
- bottleneck
- next challenge

This becomes the central visual asset of the page.

------------------------------------------------

PHASE 4 — AUTHORITY SECTION

Replace "Meet Your Guide"

New title:

After working with 120+ managers,
one pattern keeps repeating.

Content:

Explain Player Trap.

Explain Invisible Executor.

Explain why strong managers become bottlenecks.

Keep Itay visible but not central.

The framework is the hero.
Not the coach.

------------------------------------------------

PHASE 5 — INTERACTIVE SELF-ASSESSMENT

Create mini self-diagnostic before form.

Question list:

Do people wait for your approval too often?

Do you regularly jump into execution?

Do escalations land directly on your desk?

Do you feel responsible for too many decisions?

Do you work harder as your scope grows?

If user answers yes to 3+:

Highlight:

You may be operating as a Star Player,
while the organization expects a System Builder.

Replace:

Built specifically for tech managers.

SUCCESS METRIC

A visitor should understand within 5 seconds:

1. What Player Trap is.
2. Whether it might describe them.
3. Why they should take the test.

If the page cannot achieve those three outcomes,
continue simplifying.
