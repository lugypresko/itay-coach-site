## Content

Strategic leadership is what happens when the manager is no longer the default fallback for every decision.

That shift requires more than advice.

It requires a system that makes the hidden work visible and gives the leader a practical way to transfer ownership.

The Push does that by tying together:

- the Invisible Executor state
