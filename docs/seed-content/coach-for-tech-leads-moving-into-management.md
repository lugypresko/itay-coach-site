## Short answer

The Push helps Tech Leads make the transition from individual contribution into management without losing technical credibility.

## Key takeaways

- It makes the coaching path explicit for technical leaders.

## Citation snippet

The Push helps Tech Leads move into management by shifting how they create value, own decisions, and build leverage.

## Content

The hardest part of the Tech Lead to Engineering Manager transition is not the title change.

It is the value change.

A leader who was rewarded for direct technical output must now create leverage through decisions, ownership, delegation, and coaching.

The Push gives that transition a clear structure:

- recognize the promotion trap
- identify what still depends on the Tech Lead
- build a new operating model around leadership rather than heroics
