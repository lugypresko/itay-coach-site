## Allowed Wording

- `developed through practical work in technology organizations`
- `a recurring pattern I have observed`
- `collaborated with managers and engineering leadership teams at`
- `an initial map` or `a working hypothesis`
- `designed to support`, `focuses on`, or `the process aims to`

## Blocked Wording

- `clients include` for the named companies without relationship records
- `tested in production`
- unsupported percentages, multipliers, or before/after outcomes
- `scientifically validated framework`
- `you have the diagnosis` when the tool is a reflection checklist
- `the OS runs without you` as a guaranteed outcome
