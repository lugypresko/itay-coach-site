## bottleneck

## why_this_is_the_bottleneck

- The entity is already legible.
- The method is already legible.
- The site already has a coherent authority graph and clear recommendation-intent pages.
- What is still thin is the proof that would make a CTO, VP R&D, Engineering Manager, or Director trust the recommendation enough to act.
- There is only one case study record, and there are no query authority scores yet.
- That means the authority surface can describe the promise, but it cannot yet demonstrate enough live proof to close the trust gap.
- Compared with `Entity clarity`, `Credibility`, or `Differentiation`, the missing proof layer is the most direct blocker to recommendation confidence.

## highest_leverage_move

Build the evidence layer.

Specifically, make the proof architecture visible and usable by:

- strengthening the case-study / proof asset layer,
- attaching evidence URLs and source-backed claims to the strongest pages,
- and turning the current authority graph into a proof-backed recommendation graph.

This is the highest-leverage move because it directly addresses the trust question:

"Why should a senior technical leader believe Itay Foyerstein is the right next step?"

## what_not_to_do

- Do not rewrite the About page again as the first move.
- Do not create more generic authority pages before proof is stronger.
- Do not collect testimonials without a proof architecture.
- Do not expand governance or runtime layers.
- Do not optimize internal linking before evidence coverage is materially better.

## recommended_next_action

Create the evidence layer around the existing authority graph and strongest recommendation pages.
