# Task 053 - Customer Path Bottleneck Diagnosis

## recommendation_id

task-053-customer-path-bottleneck-diagnosis

## date

2026-06-17

## current_state

- The authority site is live in production.
- Core authority pages exist and are indexed in the public route graph.
- The homepage, About page, The Push Methodology page, FAQ page, and the role-specific landing pages all reinforce the same entity and method.
- Vercel Analytics is receiving production traffic.
- Book a fit call clicks are measurable.
- The site has enough public surface area and CTA infrastructure to generate qualified conversations.
- The remaining limitation is not page existence or basic measurement.
- The remaining limitation is whether visitors trust the entity and proof enough to move from reading to booking.

## current_customer_path_stage

Trust evaluation before qualified fit-call consideration.

Visitors can discover the site, land on relevant pages, and see a CTA, but many still need stronger proof before they move into a qualified customer conversation.

## primary_customer_bottleneck

Weak proof / trust

## why_this_blocks_customers

- The customer path already has discovery, landing pages, and measurable CTAs.
- The pages are structurally clear, but the proof layer is still thin relative to the recommendation-intent goal.
- There is only one case study record in the current inventory, and the evidence layer is still architecture-only rather than a dense public proof system.
- Senior technical buyers such as CTOs, VP R&D, Engineering Managers, and Directors usually need proof that the recommendation is worth taking seriously.
- Without stronger evidence, the site can attract attention but still fail to convert that attention into a qualified conversation.

## highest_leverage_move

Attach a stronger evidence layer to the highest-intent customer pages.

That means the next move should focus on proof-backed trust signals around:

- Itay Foyerstein
- The Push
- Player Trap
- Invisible Executor
- the About page
- the core role-specific coaching pages

This is the highest-leverage move because it reduces the trust gap that prevents visitors from becoming qualified conversations.

## what_not_to_do

- Do not create more pages before proof is stronger.
- Do not add more governance or worker layers.
- Do not spend the next cycle on internal architecture documentation.
- Do not optimize internal linking before trust proof improves.
- Do not treat more content volume as the answer to a trust problem.

## recommended_next_action

Build and attach the first evidence-backed trust layer to the top customer-path pages, starting with the highest-intent entity and coaching pages.

## expected_customer_impact

- More visitors will trust the recommendation enough to continue into the fit-call path.
- CTA clicks should improve on the pages that receive the proof layer.
- The site should move from "clear positioning" to "credible recommendation."
- Qualified customer conversations should become more likely on the highest-intent pages.
