## Publishing rules

1. Treat the EN and HE synergy posts as one idea with two localized URLs; never expose both as separate near-duplicate English/HE pages without canonical and language metadata.
2. Every published asset must have: direct answer, incident, two competing explanations, counterexample, one reversible experiment, scope/when-not-to-use, author proof, canonical/index/follow, and links to the commercial offer and diagnostic.
3. Every asset gets three distribution uses: indexed page/internal link, GEO answer block, and a LinkedIn/outbound follow-up. The CTA is the diagnostic; The Push is the qualified next step.
4. Use the original LinkedIn URL, date, and “adapted from a LinkedIn post” disclosure in the archive or source note. Keep claims tied to what the post actually records; do not turn generic advice into a fabricated case study.
5. Archive pages can remain accessible for credibility but should be `noindex,follow` when the intent is clearly outside the current buyer path. Do not put those URLs in the sitemap.

Week 3: publish the leadership-evidence map and decision-challenge supporting piece.
