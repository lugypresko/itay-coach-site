לא כ־H1, אלא כתזה:

Most companies treat AI as a tooling initiative. But as AI increases output, it also increases the need for review, judgment, prioritization, and governance. Without a new operating model, the engineering leader becomes the scaling limit.

זה חזק יותר מהנוסח המקורי כי הוא מחבר בין התזה לבין הסיבה שאנשים צריכים את ה־coaching שלך.

סדר הסקשנים המומלץ
1. Hero

הבעיה, הקהל, התוצאה ו־CTA אחד.

2. Recognize the pattern

לפני שאתה מסביר “למי זה מתאים”, תן למבקר לזהות את עצמו:

Decisions wait for you.
Reviews keep returning to you.
Delegation creates more follow-up, not less.
You spend the week resolving escalations instead of shaping direction.
AI increases output, but also increases review and governance load.
The team appears autonomous until something important happens.

הכותרת יכולה להיות:

You are not just managing the system. You may have become part of its critical path.

3. Why the usual fixes fail

זה סקשן קריטי שחסר בתכנית כיחידה עצמאית:

Delegating more tasks does not transfer judgment.
Hiring another manager does not automatically clarify decision rights.
Productivity tools increase output but may increase managerial load.
Better time management does not fix organizational dependency.
Generic coaching may change behavior without changing how work flows.

כאן אתה מתחיל להבדיל את עצמך מ־executive coaching כללי.

4. The leadership transition

ה־framework שלך:

Invisible Executor

The leader performs hidden work that keeps the system functioning.

Trusted Operator

The leader creates reliability, but still carries too much judgment personally.

Strategic Leader

Ownership, decisions, and escalation paths are designed into the organization.

אבל יש כאן נקודה חשובה: כרגע המעבר הזה עדיין נשמע כמו maturity model אישי. כדי להתחבר לתזה הרחבה שלך, כל שלב חייב לתאר גם את מצב המערכת, לא רק את אישיות המנהל.

לדוגמה:

Stage	Leader behavior	Organizational condition
Invisible Executor	Solves, reviews and rescues	Work depends on invisible intervention
Trusted Operator	Creates structure and reliability	Execution works, but judgment remains centralized
Strategic Leader	Designs decision capacity	Teams can act without routing everything upward
5. What changes through coaching

אל תכתוב רק “clarity, confidence and leadership”. זה גנרי.

כתוב תוצאות תפעוליות:

Clearer decision rights.
Fewer unnecessary escalations.
Ownership that survives pressure.
Better manager-to-manager operating habits.
Less rescue work.
More time for direction, organizational design and strategic decisions.
Explicit boundaries for where AI can act and where human judgment remains necessary.
6. How the process works

הייתי בונה אותו סביב עבודה אמיתית, לא סביב “sessions”:

Diagnose the dependency path
Identify where decisions, approvals and rescue work return to the leader.
Expose the hidden execution load
Make the Invisible Executor work visible.
Redesign ownership and decision rights
Decide what must move, to whom, and under what guardrails.
Apply the change in live leadership situations
Real reviews, escalations, planning conversations and management decisions.
Build a repeatable leadership operating system
Turn isolated improvements into sustained working habits.
