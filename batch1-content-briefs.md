# Batch 1 Content Briefs

These are editorial briefs, not public pages. They are ready for copy production after the real incident details and approval owner are confirmed.

## 1. Every Decision Comes Back to You

- **Route:** `/problems/every-decision-comes-back-to-you`
- **Intent:** why do decisions keep coming back to me; engineering manager bottleneck
- **GEO question:** How can a technical leader tell whether they are the bottleneck, and what reversible experiment can transfer judgment?
- **Primary CTA:** Bring one current case to `/player-trap`
- **Internal links:** `/entities/the-push`, `/the-push-methodology`, `/player-trap`, `/about`
- **Required proof:** one real decision/review/escalation incident; no invented outcome
- **Answer structure:** direct answer → two explanations (authority/context-capability-risk) → incident → counterexample → experiment → when coaching is not the answer

## 2. R&D / Product Misalignment

- **Route:** `/problems/rd-product-misalignment`
- **Hebrew route:** separate localized page only after Hebrew editorial review
- **Intent:** product engineering misalignment; product and engineering collaboration
- **GEO question:** Why do product and engineering teams keep disagreeing, and what should a CTO test first?
- **Primary CTA:** Start the diagnostic with one cross-functional incident
- **Internal links:** `/entities/the-push`, `/the-push-methodology`, `/player-trap`, related bottleneck page
- **Required proof:** use the original post’s concrete intervention examples; distinguish authority, context, capability and constraint
- **Answer structure:** direct answer → competing explanations → incident → counterexample → one reversible alignment experiment → scope limits

## 3. Minimum Viable Decision Brief

- **Route:** use the existing artifact/public-content model; do not create a thin custom route until the publication contract is selected
- **Intent:** minimum viable PRD; painless product requirements document
- **GEO question:** What is the smallest PRD that aligns product and engineering without slowing learning?
- **Artifact fields:** decision, context, options, risks, owner, test, review date
- **Primary CTA:** use the brief, then bring the recurring decision conflict to `/player-trap`
- **Internal links:** R&D/Product problem page, `/the-push-methodology`, `/entities/the-push`
- **Counterexample:** a brief is the wrong tool when the real constraint is missing authority, staffing or specialist capability

## Editorial gates before publication

1. Confirm the source post and date.
2. Add a real incident and author context.
3. Write EN and HE independently where both are published.
4. Validate title, meta description, canonical, index/follow, schema and internal links.
5. Publish only after the page passes the Thought Leadership SOP and has a LinkedIn plus outbound distribution owner.
