# LinkedIn → Website Content Workplan

## Objective

Turn the strongest LinkedIn thinking into searchable, citable assets that move a qualified reader from problem recognition to the diagnostic and then to The Push. Existing LinkedIn posts remain the source material; every public page is edited for the current buyer and the Thought Leadership SOP.

## Batch 1: publish first

1. **Every Decision Comes Back to You**
   - EN: `/problems/every-decision-comes-back-to-you`
   - HE: separate editorial equivalent only after Hebrew copy review
   - Role: flagship problem page
   - CTA: bring one current case to the diagnostic
2. **R&D/Product Misalignment**
   - EN: `/problems/product-engineering-misalignment`
   - HE: separate editorial equivalent from the Hebrew source
   - Role: cross-functional problem page
   - CTA: diagnose one recurring escalation or handoff
3. **Minimum Viable PRD**
   - URL: `/artifacts/minimum-viable-prd`
   - Role: practical artifact supporting the misalignment page
   - CTA: use the artifact, then bring the recurring decision conflict

## Batch 2: authority and adjacent demand

- `/insights/what-an-agile-coach-actually-does`
- `/methodology/leadership-judgment`
- `/methodology/strategy-as-tests`
- `/insights/assessing-product-judgment`

Publish Batch 2 only after Batch 1 has an owner for LinkedIn distribution and outbound follow-up.

## Required page standard

Every page must contain a direct answer, two competing explanations, one real incident, a counter-example, an artifact/table/template, clear scope boundaries, author/proof, self-referencing canonical, `index,follow`, one H1, suitable schema, and three contextual internal links. English and Hebrew pages are edited independently; no machine-translated twins.

## Distribution and measurement

For each published asset: add an internal link from an existing relevant page, publish one LinkedIn follow-up, use the asset in one real outbound conversation, and link to the diagnostic with UTM attribution. Track visits, diagnostic starts, completion rate, qualified conversations, assisted opportunities, non-branded impressions/clicks, and GEO mentions.

## Hold list

Keep customer retention, eCommerce conversion, inbound marketing, marketing Scrum Master, microservices, interview preparation, generic KPIs, and startup/funding posts in an archive. Reconsider only if the ICP or offer expands.

## Definition of Done

- Source post and editorial brief are linked.
- Page is live on the intended route with correct metadata and schema.
- Page passes the SOP evidence checklist.
- At least three internal links and one diagnostic CTA work.
- EN/HE language decision is recorded.
- LinkedIn distribution and one outbound use are completed.
- Analytics source and assisted-conversation attribution are recorded.
- URL, screenshot, and validation result are saved with the task.
