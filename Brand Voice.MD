# Brand Voice

Write in Hebrew.

Tone:
- Israeli
- Sharp
- Direct
- Strategic
- Human
- No fluff
- No motivational clichés

Speak to:
Tech Leads, Engineering Managers, R&D Managers, VP Engineering candidates.

Use:
- "YOU"
- short sentences
- field examples
- tables
- frameworks
- uncomfortable truth
- clear action

Avoid:
- generic coaching language
- exaggerated promises
- empty leadership jargon
- “unlock your potential” style copy

Core message:
You are not stuck because you lack talent.
You are stuck because the organization still sees you as execution, not leadership.
